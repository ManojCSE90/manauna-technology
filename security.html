Security Governance

Security & Responsible Disclosure

Last Updated: November 7, 2026

Security and privacy are core architectural tenets at Manauna Technology. We welcome responsible security researchers to report potential vulnerabilities.

1. Security Philosophy

Manauna Technology follows defense-in-depth principles across all mobile client applications, developer utilities, and web endpoints. We utilize strict transport security (HTTPS/TLS 1.3), compile-time code obfuscation, automated dependency scanning, and zero unnecessary telemetry storage.

2. Responsible Disclosure Program

If you discover a security vulnerability in any application or web asset owned by Manauna Technology, we ask that you disclose it to us confidentially before making any public disclosure.

Security Contact Email: security@manaunatechnology.com
Standard Response Time: Within 48 hours
RFC 9116 File: /.well-known/security.txt

3. Guidelines for Researchers

When investigating potential vulnerabilities, we request that you:

  • Do not violate the privacy of our users or alter/delete user data.
  • Do not perform Denial of Service (DoS/DDoS) attacks against our infrastructure.
  • Allow us a reasonable period of time to remediate the issue prior to public disclosure.
  • Provide clear steps or proof of concept to help our engineering team reproduce the issue.

4. Application Signing & Verification

All Android APKs and bundles published by Manauna Technology on Google Play are signed with verified cryptographic keys managed through Google Play App Signing. Always ensure you download our official applications directly from the Google Play Store or verified official channels.
