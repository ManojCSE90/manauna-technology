2. Information We Collect

We adhere to strict data minimization principles. We only collect the minimal information necessary to deliver and improve our software applications:

  • Device & Diagnostics Data: Device model, operating system version, anonymous crash logs, and non-identifiable performance telemetry to diagnose software instability.
  • User-Provided Feedback: Communications, inquiries, or bug reports submitted directly through email or contact forms.
  • Application-Specific Local Data: For offline utilities and launchers (e.g. AILauncher), user customizations, layout preferences, and app categorization settings are retained locally on your device and are never sent to external servers unless you explicitly configure cloud backups.

3. How We Use Information

We use collected data solely for legitimate operational purposes:

  • To operate, debug, and maintain application functionality.
  • To respond to customer service requests and security notices.
  • To safeguard against unauthorized access, fraudulent activities, or malicious attacks.
  • To ensure compliance with applicable laws and platform policies (such as Google Play Developer Policies).

We do NOT sell, lease, or rent your personal data to any third parties or data brokers.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy & Limited Use Disclosure

Manauna Technology's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically:

  • We do not use Google user data to serve advertisements, build user profiles for advertising, or retarget users.
  • Human interaction with user data is strictly prohibited unless required to resolve critical security incidents, requested explicitly by the user, or mandated by applicable law.
  • Data obtained through Google APIs is restricted exclusively to providing or improving user-facing features that are prominent in the requesting application's user interface.

5. Third-Party Services & SDKs

Our applications may integrate standard, industry-vetted SDKs for crash logging and platform analytics. All such vendors are required to maintain strict data protection standards:

  • Google Play Services: Core application infrastructure and Google Play licensing.
  • Firebase Crashlytics (Google LLC): Aggregated crash analysis for crash remediation.

Each third-party service operates in compliance with their respective privacy policies.

6. Data Retention and Account / Data Deletion Requests

In compliance with Google Play's Data Safety policies and international privacy laws, users have the right to request deletion of any personal data we may hold.

How to Request Account & Data Deletion

To initiate complete removal of your records, submit a request specifying your registered email or device identifier:

Subject Line: Data Deletion Request - [Your Name / App Name]

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our general services and applications are not directed towards children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately so we can take steps to remove such information.

8. Security Safeguards

Manauna Technology employs administrative, physical, and technical safeguards designed to protect personal data from accidental loss, misuse, and unauthorized access or disclosure. All web communications utilize modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.2+ / HTTPS).
